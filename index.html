Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about BizTrack.

What is BizTrack?

BizTrack is a simple expense and profit tracker for small businesses. You record income and expenses, and the dashboard shows what came in, what went out and what you kept.

Is BizTrack free?

You can create an account and start tracking for free. Some features, such as Reports and CSV export, are part of Premium.

Where is my data stored?

Your data is stored in your own browser on your device, not on a BizTrack server. Because it is not backed up elsewhere, export it regularly.

Which currencies and languages does BizTrack support?

BizTrack supports currencies for countries across Africa, the Americas, Europe and Asia, and is available in English, French, Spanish, Portuguese and Arabic.

Can I install BizTrack on my phone?

Yes. BizTrack is an installable web app. Open it in your phone's browser and choose Add to Home Screen, or use the install prompt.
