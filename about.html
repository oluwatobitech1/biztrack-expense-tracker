About BizTrack

BizTrack is a simple expense and profit tracker for small businesses. It was built by Oluwatobi Ayodele, a web developer who works with small business owners and saw how many of them track money in notebooks, chat messages or memory.

What BizTrack does

  • Records income and expenses with category and date.
  • Shows profit on a clear dashboard.
  • Supports many currencies and five languages: English, French, Spanish, Portuguese and Arabic.
  • Installs on a phone like an app.
  • Keeps your data in your own browser, not on our servers.

Our goal

To make bookkeeping light enough that any business owner can keep up with it. We also publish free guides on tracking expenses, understanding profit and pricing.
