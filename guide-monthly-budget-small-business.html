How to Create a Simple Monthly Budget for Your Small Business

By Oluwatobi Ayodele · Updated October 2026

A budget is a plan for your money. It does not have to be complicated. A one-page monthly budget is enough to avoid many cash problems.

Step 1: Look at the last three months

Add up your income and expenses for the past three months. Averages are more reliable than a single month, because they smooth out unusual sales or one-off costs.

Step 2: Estimate next month income

Be careful and realistic. If your sales vary, use the lower end of your usual range. It is better to be pleasantly surprised than short of cash.

Step 3: List fixed costs

These are costs that stay about the same: rent, wages, internet, loan repayments and subscriptions. Fixed costs must be covered first.

Step 4: List variable costs

Stock, transport, packaging, airtime and delivery change with sales. Use your past averages as a guide, and set a limit for each category.

Step 5: Set aside savings

Plan a small amount each month for emergencies, repairs and slow seasons. Even 5 percent of income builds a useful cushion over a year.

Step 6: Compare every week

Each week, check actual spending against the budget. If a category is running high, adjust early rather than at month end.

A simple template

Write four lines: expected income, fixed costs, variable costs and savings. What remains is what you can safely spend on growth or take as your own pay. If the number is negative, reduce a cost or raise your income target.

Review your budget every month and improve it as you learn. After three or four months you will find it much easier to predict.
