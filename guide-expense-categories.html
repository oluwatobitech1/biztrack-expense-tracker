Business Expense Categories Explained, With Examples

By Oluwatobi Ayodele · Updated October 2026

Good categories turn a long list of payments into clear information. These are the ones most small businesses need.

Cost of goods or materials

Items you buy to make or resell: fabric for a tailor, ingredients for a food seller, stock for a shop.

Transport and delivery

Fuel, fares, dispatch riders and delivery fees paid for business purposes.

Rent and utilities

Shop or office rent, electricity, water, internet and generator fuel.

Wages and labour

Salaries, daily pay for helpers and commissions paid to sales staff.

Marketing

Adverts, flyers, printing, branded packaging and social media promotion.

Equipment and repairs

Machines, phones, tools and the cost of maintaining them.

Fees and charges

Bank charges, payment processing fees, licences and permits.

Other

Anything that fits nowhere else. If this category grows large, create a new one.

Tips

  • Use the same names every time.
  • Do not count personal spending.
  • Review categories every few months.

Tax rules differ by country, so check with a local professional about which expenses are deductible.
